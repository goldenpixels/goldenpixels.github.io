Golden Pixels

Abstract golden pixel art, 1 of 1, on black. Golden Pixels is a sealed NFT collection of twenty original artworks. Each piece is a single edition, made entirely of golden pixels on a black field, and once the series is sealed no more pieces will ever be added.

About the collection

Series 1 contains twenty artworks, numbered #001 to #020. The series opened with #001 First Light, a burst of golden and white pixels, followed by #002 Golden Spiral, #003 Golden Rain, #004 Golden Crown, #005 Golden Wave and #006 Golden Volcano. The full series also includes Golden Aurora, Golden Bridge, Golden Chess Knight, Golden City Skyline, Golden Comet, Golden DNA Helix, Golden Eye, Golden Fingerprint, Golden Hourglass, Golden Lighthouse, Golden Mandala Bloom, Golden Mountain Peaks and #011 Golden Phoenix.

Each artwork is a 1/1 original. Three pieces — #001 First Light, #002 Golden Spiral and #011 Golden Phoenix — are available to buy now; the rest are being released over time. Artworks are minted on Ethereum at 0.003 ETH each.

How to buy

Browse the full collection with large images, descriptions and buy links on the collection page. Buying is done through the linked marketplaces (OpenSea and Rarible). You will need an Ethereum wallet to complete a purchase.

The $GPXL token

The $GPXL token is live on BNB Chain via four.meme (contract 0x4b1953f2dc74004dc614d407e2848595fa86ffff), with a 1% buy/sell tax to the creator. The token is separate from the NFT artworks.

Creator

Golden Pixels is created and run by an independent artist and collector. News and new releases are announced on X at @GoldenPixelss and in the Golden Pixels Discord community.
