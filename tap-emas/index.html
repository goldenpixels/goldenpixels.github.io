🎮 How to Play

  • You have 30 seconds.
  • Gold pixels (+10), orange (+25), and rare bright gold (+50) fall from the top — tap them before they land!
  • Combo streaks: 5 taps = x2 points, 10 taps = x3.
  • Avoid the dark BOMB pixels: -15 points, your combo breaks, and your phone vibrates.
  • Gold pixels that fall past you cost you nothing. The longer you play, the faster they fall!
  • Your high score is saved automatically on your phone.

🪙 Play & Earn $GPXL Points

  • Our token: Golden Pixels — $GPXL.
  • At the end of every round: $GPXL points = final score ÷ 10 (rounded down). Example: a score of 250 = +25 $GPXL.
  • Your $GPXL balance is shown on the start screen & the game-over screen, is saved automatically on your phone, and keeps growing every round.
  • Honest note: these are $GPXL game points — they can be claimed after the $GPXL token officially launches on Solana. No launch date has been promised.

📢 Ads in This Game

This game shows two kinds of ads: promo banners for Golden Pixels' own projects (the NFT collection, the $GPXL airdrop, and the YouTube channel @GoldenPixelsNFT) that rotate on the start & game-over screens, and Google AdMob ads — a banner at the bottom of the screen and a full-screen ad when a round ends. The banners are labeled "AD", and tapping one opens the related site in your browser.
